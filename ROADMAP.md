# Roadmap

Items are planned or under consideration, not commitments.

1. **Planned:** automatic host-code tuning-scope discovery, with payoff/tractability ranking and safety classification.
2. **Planned:** interactive clarification that resolves assumptions and open questions.
3. **Planned:** safe run mode with sandboxing, resource caps, noise probing, initial surrogate checks, and interleaved verification.
4. **Under consideration:** evidence-backed patch/PR generation under strict allowlisted paths and unchanged-dependency policy.
5. **Planned:** tested BoTorch and SMAC3 adapters; more backends, multi-fidelity, multi-objective, and contextual tuning.
6. **Under consideration:** domain packs for HPC libraries, GPU kernels, collectives, MD/PDE, databases, compiler flags, and LLM serving.
7. **Planned:** meta-benchmark suite comparing rule-selected methods with strong simple baselines.
8. **Planned:** community rule packs and reviewed citation promotion.
9. **Under consideration:** MCP and IDE-agent integration.
10. **Under consideration:** other community priorities.

Also planned: safe hosted/local LLM characterization with explicit payload review, redaction and replay; research-provider provenance; process-group-safe real evaluation on supported platforms; robust paired statistical verification; richer code-derived symbolic resolution; and examples driven by mocked evaluators in CI.
